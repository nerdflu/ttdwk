## Scope and mode

Selected place-detail routes are a Read/Experience surface: parents confirm whether an outing is practical while location media carries the sense of discovery.

## Audience, job and content

Local parents and carers need the place, cost, age fit, timing, location, current-condition warning and external map/official actions within seconds. Editorial notes use existing verified place fields. Ratings, reviews and unsupported proof are never invented. Demonstration media must be conspicuously labelled and replaced before launch.

## Direction

Desktop retains the square brand and city-navigation rail, removes the permanent right rail, and gives the main canvas to a compact sticky story panel beside a dominant media sequence. The gallery should visually track the story's height. Newsletter, advertising and related paths follow the complete spotlight. Mobile compresses city navigation into one horizontal row, then orders title, summary, four facts, lead media, actions, deeper notes, remaining media and downstream modules.

The memorable moment is the calm square brand rail framing a large, natural place gallery without squeezing practical information.

## Constraints and unresolved decisions

Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA, Australian English, city context, explicit external-link behaviour, responsive images and reduced-motion support. The production coming-soon gate must keep concealed content inert. Dynamic gallery fields, structured hours and prices, visit duration, accessibility details, `has_page` routing and the final real logo remain future content-model work.
