# Product Direction

## Not a Google Maps Clone
ThingsToDoWithKids.com.au is a simple, city-led discovery engine, not just a directory of coordinates or a giant event directory.
We want to inspire parents with curated ideas, mini-adventures, hidden gems, and simple weekend plans rather than just listing every playground in existence.

## Mix of Content
By mixing Places, Ideas, and Guides, we provide actionable, durable SEO value. 
An "Idea" like "Rockpool hunting before lunch" is more valuable to a parent than just a pin on a map for "Burleigh Heads Rockpools".
City, Category, Place, Idea, and Guide pages form the durable SEO backbone of the platform.

## Event Strategy
Events are treated as lightweight external listings for freshness and usefulness, **not** for mass page generation.
- We intentionally avoid generating internal event pages (/[city]/events/[slug]/).
- We are not a Facebook events replacement or a directory for every baby rhyme time or small council class.
- Events focus on bigger/noteworthy things in major cities (e.g. festivals, surf comps, major markets).
- Event cards simply link out to the official source for details. This reduces maintenance risk when events change or cancel.
- The site takes no responsibility for cancellations or changed info.

## Operational Goal
Long-term, the platform should be heavily automated.
Bots and AI agents will scan trusted sources and emails to queue up content in Google Sheets.
A human editor simply approves or rejects rows, and the static site rebuilds daily.
